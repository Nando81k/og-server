THE OGs SERVER · HANDBOOK

You found the spot.

This started as a group chat. It outgrew the group chat. Now it's here — still a friend group that happens to have a server, not a server trying to become a friend group. Make yourself at home.

The rules

Five of them, and they're mostly common sense. Read once, then forget it.

Be good to people. Racism, homophobia, transphobia, bigotry of any flavor — that's the one we don't negotiate on. Everything else we can talk through.
Talk your trash, keep it friendly. Competitive needling is half the point of #2k and #madden. If someone's actually had enough, let it go — reading the room is the whole skill.
What's said here stays here. Don't screenshot the server into other group chats. #highlights is the stuff we're happy to have travel.
Nobody's business is content. Don't share someone's @, their job, where they live, or what they're going through without asking first.
#smoke-lounge is 18+. Grab the role in onboarding. That one's not ours to bend.

The first rule and the age rule are the two we can't bend on — they're what keeps this a place people want to be. Everything else is a conversation.

Start here

Four things, about a minute total. None of it is a test.

  1. Answer the onboarding questions. That's what gets you your games, your borough, and the channels that come with them.
  2. Say hi in #general-chat whenever you're ready. No pressure, and nobody's going to make it weird.
  3. Put your borough in #irl-plans if you want to actually link up. A lot of plans stall out because nobody knows who's close.
  4. #bodega-tier-list is a serious academic channel and will be treated as such.

After a week you're promoted from New Member to Member automatically. Nobody has to approve it.

Commands

Type / in any channel and Discord shows you these. The ones marked MOD are refused for everyone else.

OG Bot

/lfg <game> [slots]Start a session. Pings everyone with that game's role and links the voice room. Defaults to 5 slots.
/picksYour private link to this week's NFL pick'em.
/leaderboardSeason standings on demand, counting only weeks that have actually been scored.
/award <user> <points> <reason>Hand out season points for anything the pick'em can't score. Public on purpose.MOD

Running a tournament

/bracket create <name>Open sign-ups.MOD
/bracket joinPut your name in.
/bracket leaveTake it out, before it starts.
/bracket startDraw the bracket. The draw is random.MOD
/bracket viewWho plays who right now.
/bracket report <match> <winner>Say who won your set. Start typing a name and it offers the sets waiting on a result. Either player can report it.
/bracket undoTake back the last result. Works up until the grand final is reported.MOD
/bracket cancelCall it off. Nobody gets points.MOD

Double elimination — you have to lose twice to be out. Best of 3 until winners and losers finals, then best of 5. If the player coming out of losers wins the grand final, you play a second one, because they had to lose twice to get there and the other player hasn't lost at all. Report when your set ends — everyone behind you in the bracket is waiting on that result.

Music

/play <song>Quick play. Join a voice room first.
/search <song>Use this when the title is ambiguous — it returns a clickable list with each result's artist and duration, which is how you tell Wiz Khalifa's "See You Again" from Miley Cyrus's.

Chip also has queue tools, filters, lyrics, DJ roles and 24/7 mode. There are three copies — Chip, Chip 2, Chip 3 — so if one is busy in another room, call the next one. Neither Chip nor any other music bot suggests song names as you type; /search is the substitute.

Season points

One leaderboard fed by everything: the pick'em, tournaments, and anything a mod decides was worth points. It lives in #season-leaderboard.

The pick'em

NFL, every week. You rank your picks by confidence — most confident gets the most points, least gets one — so a week with 16 games is worth up to about 136. /picks gets your link. Picks lock at the first kickoff of the week.

A week is scored only once every game in it is final, which is why the board stays quiet mid-week. That's deliberate, not broken.

Everything else

Meme of the week, aux battles, fantasy finishes — a mod runs /award. The ledger is append-only: a correction is a second entry, never an edit, so the board can always be explained by reading the entries that built it.

Moderation

What gets deleted on sight, and what gets logged.

Automod deletes

invite linksDiscord invites to other servers.
6+ mentions / 10sMass ping.
10+ messages / 5sSpam.

There is no caps filter and no word filter, on purpose. A caps filter on a server whose main room is #2k would fire twenty times a night. People should be able to talk normally.

Logged to #warn-log

Deletes, edits, purges, joins and leaves, role changes, nickname and avatar changes, bans, unbans and timeouts. Voice events are off — with eight standing voice rooms they'd bury everything else.
