# The `#welcome-rules` pin

This one is **not** posted by the seeder, on purpose. Every other channel guide
comes from OG Bot, but a welcome and a set of rules read differently coming
from a bot than from a person — and the whole point of this text is that
someone is glad you turned up. So it stays a hand-posted message from the
owner's own account, which is why `welcome-rules` is in `ALREADY_PINNED`.

Kept here so it is version-controlled and reviewable like everything else.
To change it: edit the pinned message in Discord and paste the text below.

---

you found the spot

This started as a group chat. It outgrew the group chat. Now it's here — still
a friend group that happens to have a server, not a server trying to become a
friend group. Make yourself at home.

**the rules**

**Be good to people.**
Racism, homophobia, transphobia, bigotry of any flavor — that's the one we
don't negotiate on. Everything else we can talk through.

**Talk your trash, keep it friendly.**
Competitive needling is half the point of ⁠🏀┃2k and ⁠🏈┃madden. If someone's
actually had enough, let it go. Reading the room is the whole skill.

**What's said here stays here.**
Don't screenshot the server into other group chats. ⁠⭐┃highlights is the stuff
we're happy to have travel.

**Nobody's business is content.**
Don't share someone's @, their job, where they live, or what they're going
through without asking first.

**⁠💨┃smoke-lounge is 18+.**
Grab the role in onboarding. That one's not ours to bend.

**about OG**

OG means you were in the group chat before the server went public. That's the
whole definition — it's a timestamp, not a rank, and nobody in here is above
anybody.

Everyone else earns Veteran just by sticking around and being someone people
are glad to see. It comes on its own.

Glad you're here. Go play something.

## What changed, and why

The previous version opened with "No weirdos", warned that bigotry was
"removed the first time, not the third", and told people "don't take it
personal". Every one of those is *correct policy* written as a threat — the
first thing a new person read was a list of ways to get thrown out.

The rules themselves did not change. Only who they sound like they were
written for: someone joining, rather than someone about to cause a problem.
The bigotry rule is still absolute and still first, because that is the one
that actually protects people. It just no longer greets everyone as a suspect.
